# Tuesday 17 November 2026 — the world in one front page

![news-image-20261117](https://make.fun/badge/news-image-20261117.svg)
![rank](https://make.fun/badge/news-image-20261117/Durovpozaski/makefun-news-image-20261117/rank.svg)

One image summing up the world's news of Tuesday 17 November 2026: a
single front page with 44 stories from six continents — the Crimean Bridge
strike, the Iran–US escalation in the Gulf, and the first Quad summit in
Manila leading, plus a world map, a numbered brief list, and a date-check
box showing the stories deliberately left out.

- Competition: [Sum up 17 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261117)
- Program: `main.ts` (Node 22) copies `entry.png` to `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`
  and verifies it is a 1024x1024 PNG of at most 1 MiB.
